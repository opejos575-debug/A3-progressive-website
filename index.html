    Our Mission

    To provide quality middle school education that develops academic ability, discipline, confidence, creativity and responsible leadership in our students.

    About Our Middle School

    A Strong Foundation for Future Success

    At A3 Progressive Basic Academy, our Middle School provides students with a supportive and engaging learning environment where they can develop strong academic skills, discover their talents and prepare confidently for the next stage of their education.

    We combine effective classroom teaching with practical activities, technology, creativity, discipline and character development to help our students become knowledgeable, responsible and confident young people.

    Academic Excellence

    We provide quality instruction across our middle school subjects and encourage students to develop strong study habits and critical thinking skills.

    Character Development

    Students are encouraged to demonstrate discipline, respect, honesty, responsibility and good conduct both in and outside the classroom.

    Skills & Practical Learning

    We expose students to practical learning, digital skills, creativity and problem-solving activities that prepare them for the future.

    Leadership & Responsibility

    Our students are given opportunities to build confidence, teamwork, communication skills and leadership qualities.

    Heads Of The School

    Meet the dedicated leaders guiding A3 Progressive Basic Academic towards academic excellence.

    FOUNDER

    Prof. Aminat Toyosi

    Prof. Aminat Toyosi is the founder of A3 Progressive Basic Academy, which was established in 2014.

    She is also described as a Chief Lecturer in Physical and Health Education (PHE) in FCT (Bichi), Kano State. Her background in Physical and Health Education reflects a strong interest in education, personal development, physical well-being, discipline, and the holistic development of learners.

    Through the establishment of A3 Progressive Basic Academy, Prof. Aminat Toyosi has demonstrated a commitment to providing children with an educational foundation that goes beyond classroom instruction.

    2014 Founder PHE
