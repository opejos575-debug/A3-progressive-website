NURSERY PROGRAMME

Nursery Subjects

Our Nursery programme provides engaging subjects and activities that support children's early development, creativity, confidence and learning skills.

English Language

Speaking, listening, vocabulary, reading and simple communication skills.

Mathematics

Counting, numbers, simple addition, shapes, patterns and early mathematical activities.

Phonics

Letter sounds, pronunciation, word recognition and early reading development.

Basic Science

Learning about plants, animals, the body, weather and the world around us.

Quantitative Reasoning

Developing children's ability to think, compare numbers, identify patterns and solve simple problems.

Verbal Reasoning

Building vocabulary, word understanding, matching, grouping and simple language activities.

Creative Arts

Drawing, colouring, painting, crafts and other activities that encourage creativity.

Social Habits

Good manners, sharing, cooperation, friendship and responsible behaviour.

Civic Education

Learning about rules, respect, responsibilities, community and good citizenship.

Computer Studies

Introduction to computers, basic technology, devices and digital awareness.

Physical Education

Movement, balance, coordination, games and activities that support physical development.

Health Education

Personal hygiene, healthy habits, cleanliness, safety and basic health awareness.

Religious & Moral Education

Learning kindness, honesty, respect and positive moral values.

Rhymes & Songs

Songs, nursery rhymes and musical activities that support memory, language and expression.

Handwriting

Pencil control, letter formation, tracing and early handwriting practice.

Writing Skills

Developing early writing through letters, words, drawing and simple sentence activities.

Number Work

Number recognition, counting, matching and simple number activities.

Practical Life Skills

Self-care, responsibility, organisation and simple everyday activities.

Environmental Studies

Exploring the home, school, community, nature and the environment.

Home Economics

Introduction to food, clothing, cleanliness, home care and simple domestic activities.
