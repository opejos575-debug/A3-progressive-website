OUR STORY

Education That Builds The Future

A3 Progressive Basic Academy is an educational institution established in 2014 by Prof. Aminat Toyosi with a strong commitment to providing children with quality education, sound moral training, practical knowledge and the skills necessary to succeed in an ever-changing world.

The school was founded on the understanding that education should do more than prepare children for examinations. It should help learners develop confidence, discipline, creativity, responsibility, good manners and the ability to apply knowledge to everyday situations.
